Features

Charts, depth, execution and discipline in one app

TickSync runs on your Windows desktop alongside DAS Trader Pro, using your own IQFeed market data. The download also includes TickSync Scanner and TickSync Analyzer.

  • Real-time tick charts

    Tick-by-tick price charts that update live from the IQFeed Level 1 feed.

  • Candle charts, seconds to daily

    Timeframes from seconds up to daily, with VWAP, EMAs and an RVOL (relative volume) row.

  • Volume profile

    Session POC, VAH and VAL, prior-day levels, and a rolling value area over the last N bars.

  • Footprint and delta

    Volume traded at the bid and ask inside each candle, shown as net delta or bid × ask.

  • Level 2 and Time & Sales

    Market depth and the tape side by side, from IQFeed Nasdaq Level II data.

  • News, earnings and halts

    Badges on the chart for news headlines, earnings dates and trading halts on the symbol you are watching.

  • DAS Trader hotkey integration

    Works with DAS Trader Pro hotkeys behind safety interlocks, including cancel-first exits and exit protection. During the beta, automatic exits and entry lines run in Audit: they show what they would send and send nothing.

  • Trade lines on the chart

    Armed entry lines, hard stops and take-profit lines, drawn directly on the chart.

  • TickSync Scanner

    Watches the whole U.S. market for names that are actually moving, and loads any of them into TickSync’s charts with one click.

  • TickSync Analyzer

    Reviews the sessions TickSync records and writes a plain-language report, with an optional package to discuss with ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini.

Rules and discipline tools

Rules you set ahead of the session, checked by the app as you trade.

  • Trading windowsDefine the times of day you trade, with a guard for entries outside them.
  • No averaging downA guard against adding to a losing position.
  • Post-loss cooldownA pause on new entries after a losing trade.
  • Minimum risk per shareA minimum distance between entry and stop for new trades.
  • Pre-trade questionnaireA short checklist to answer before entries are enabled for a trading window.

Requirements

What you need

  • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
  • Your own DTN IQFeed subscription — for market data. Level 2 is optional. Start IQFeed and log in before opening TickSync. The scanner and TickSync’s charts share your IQFeed symbol limit.
  • About 20 GB of free disk space — TickSync keeps the last 5 trading days of recorded session data and deletes older days.
  • DAS Trader Pro — optional. The charts, the scanner and the Analyzer work with any broker, or none; numpad hotkeys and symbol sync need DAS.
  • A beta key from the developer — see Beta access.

Your accounts, your data

TickSync connects to your own IQFeed and DAS Trader accounts. It does not provide market data or brokerage services; those subscriptions are with DTN and your broker.

Install

Install in 4 steps

  1. Download

    Download the zip from the latest release.

  2. Extract

    Extract the zip to a folder (right-click it, then Extract All).

  3. Run TickSync.exe

    Open the extracted folder and run TickSync.exe. TickSync Scanner and TickSync Analyzer are in the same folder: open them from TickSync’s Menu, or run them directly.

    If Windows SmartScreen says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info then Run anyway.

    This appears because the beta is not code-signed yet.

  4. Enter your beta key

    Paste your beta key when the app asks for it.
